Support

Questions, problems or ideas: we’d love to hear from you.

Email support@glidestash.com. We usually reply within a couple of days. If something isn’t working, tell us your Mac model, your macOS version and what you were doing.

Common questions

Why does GlideStash need Accessibility access?

macOS only lets apps list other apps’ windows and bring them forward with Accessibility access. GlideStash uses it for its tabs, shortcuts and layouts, and never to read what you type. Turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

What happens when my free trial ends?

GlideStash keeps working as the free version. The tab bar, the paid widgets and anything past the free version’s limits are greyed out with an Unlock button. Nothing is deleted: when you unlock GlideStash, everything comes back as it was.

How do I enter my license key?

Your key is in the email you got after buying. In GlideStash, click any Unlock button, or open Settings › General, and enter it there. GlideStash checks it with our payment provider once, and everything is unlocked.

How do I move my license to a new Mac?

One purchase works on up to three Macs at once. To free one up, open GlideStash on the old Mac, go to Settings › General and choose Remove License from This Mac, then enter your key on the new Mac. If the old Mac is gone, email us and we’ll free it up for you.

I lost my license key.

Search your email for your GlideStash receipt from Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider: the key is in it. You can also find your orders at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders with the email you bought with. Still stuck? Email us.

What does “Sync with the Reminders app” do?

It makes the Reminders app the home for your GlideStash reminders. Turn it on in GlideStash’s Settings › Schedule (it’s part of the full version, and GlideStash asks for access to your reminders).

While it’s on, any reminder you add to GlideStash’s schedule, from the sidebar’s Scheduled Today, becomes a real reminder in your default Reminders list, with its time and repeat days. The Reminders app alerts you when it’s due, on your iPhone and other devices too through iCloud, even when GlideStash isn’t running. Today’s reminders, and overdue ones, from all your lists show in Scheduled Today, where the circle checks one off.

Turning it on also moves the reminders already in GlideStash’s schedule into the Reminders app, except ones set to run at startup or turned off, which stay in GlideStash. Turning it off later leaves your reminders in the Reminders app; new ones you add in GlideStash stay in GlideStash again.
